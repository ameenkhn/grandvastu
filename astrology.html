Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Do I need exact birth time for an accurate reading?
An accurate birth time helps, but our practitioners can rectify uncertain timings using key life events. Send what you know and we will refine.
Are remedies expensive or hard to perform?
No. We favour ethical, daily-life remedies first — mantra, charity, dietary tweaks — and only suggest gemstones or pujas when truly indicated.
Can readings be done online?
Yes. Most clients are abroad. Sessions take place over Zoom or Google Meet, and the written report is delivered by email.
How long is a session and what does it cover?
Sessions run 60-90 minutes for life readings, 45 minutes for focused questions. We cover health, career, marriage, finance and any specific concerns you bring.
