Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How quickly will I hear back?
Within 24 hours on every weekday and most weekends. Calls and WhatsApp during office hours (09:30 – 19:00 IST) usually receive a same-hour response.
Do I need to visit in person, or can it be done online?
Most consultations — astrology, numerology, tarot, energy healing, courses — are delivered online with video calls. Vastu site assessments can be done remotely from architectural drawings, or in person at the property when feasible.
What languages do you offer sessions in?
We work fluently in Hindi, English and Punjabi. Translators can be arranged on request for other Indian languages.
Is the first call free?
Yes. Your first 15-minute discovery call is complimentary — we use it to understand your situation and recommend the right path. There is no obligation to proceed.
How are fees structured?
Each service has its own fee, shared transparently in the proposal we send after the discovery call. No hidden charges, and we never recommend remedies you don't need.
Can I refer a family member or friend?
Absolutely. Most of our clients come through trusted recommendations. If you'd like, we can co-ordinate a session that includes the family member directly.
